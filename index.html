05 How does it fit on a microcontroller?

Bigger isn't better. Built for the job is.

General-purpose AI carries everything it might ever be asked, so it needs gigabytes and a data centre. Ours carries only what the product needs: trained for one job, sized for the chip, taking turns on one accelerator. On that job, it beats models 18 to 60 times its size.

09 Foundation · keyword spotting · ACTIVATION

The model learns sounds, not keywords.

Our keyword spotter is taught the speech sounds of a language rather than a fixed list of keywords, so a keyword or a command is just text: a new one needs no recordings, no retraining and no reflash. One lineage then takes a product from its first prototype to a wake word on a battery.
